What defines the Outlaw Country movement of the 1970s?

Led by Waylon Jennings, Willie Nelson, Johnny Cash, and Kris Kristofferson, Outlaw Country broke away from slick Nashville studio production in favor of raw, honest storytelling, driving rhythms, and complete creative freedom.
